Scaffold Hopping in Drug Discovery : Finding New Chemical Frameworks for Known Targets

When a drug candidate has the right biological activity but poor drug-like properties, or when intellectual property constraints prevent the use of a known scaffold, scaffold hopping in drug discovery offers a solution. Scaffold hopping in drug discovery involves replacing the core chemical framework of an active compound with a different scaffold that maintains the key pharmacophoric features while potentially improving properties such as solubility, metabolic stability, or selectivity. Scaffold hopping in drug discovery is a powerful strategy for generating novel intellectual property and overcoming development challenges.


Computational methods have made scaffold hopping in drug discovery faster and more systematic. By using pharmacophore models, shape similarity searches, and AI-based molecular generation, researchers can rapidly identify alternative scaffolds that preserve the binding interactions of the original compound while offering new chemical diversity.

What Is Scaffold Hopping in Drug Discovery?

Scaffold hopping in drug discovery refers to the process of replacing the central core structure of a bioactive molecule with a different chemical framework while retaining the key pharmacophoric features responsible for biological activity. Scaffold hopping in drug discovery can involve replacing ring systems, introducing bioisosteres, or using entirely different molecular architectures that present the same binding groups in the same spatial arrangement. The goal is to find new chemical matter that maintains activity while improving properties or creating novel intellectual property.

  • Core ring system replacement with alternative scaffolds
  • Bioisostere introduction for property improvement
  • Shape-based scaffold hopping using 3D similarity
  • AI-guided scaffold generation for novel IP

Computational Methods for Scaffold Hopping in Drug Discovery

Computational scaffold hopping in drug discovery uses pharmacophore-based virtual screening to find compounds with the same binding features but different scaffolds. Shape similarity tools like ROCS compare the 3D shape and electrostatic properties of molecules to identify scaffold hops. Generative AI models can design entirely new scaffolds that satisfy pharmacophore constraints. Fragment-based approaches also support scaffold hopping in drug discovery by identifying new core fragments that can replace existing ones.

  • Pharmacophore-based virtual screening for scaffold hops
  • 3D shape similarity searching with ROCS
  • Generative AI for novel scaffold design
  • Fragment-based scaffold replacement strategies

Benefits of Scaffold Hopping in Drug Discovery

Scaffold hopping in drug discovery enables the generation of novel intellectual property by creating structurally distinct compounds with similar biological activity. It provides opportunities to improve drug-like properties such as solubility, metabolic stability, and selectivity that may be limited by the original scaffold. Scaffold hopping in drug discovery also helps overcome resistance mechanisms that are scaffold-specific.

  • Novel intellectual property generation
  • Improved drug-like properties through scaffold change
  • Overcoming scaffold-specific resistance mechanisms
  • Access to new chemical space around known targets

Future of Scaffold Hopping in Drug Discovery

The future of scaffold hopping in drug discovery includes AI systems that can automatically identify and evaluate scaffold hops, integration with synthesis planning tools to assess synthetic accessibility, and generative models that design scaffolds optimized for multiple properties simultaneously. As AI tools improve, scaffold hopping in drug discovery will become faster and more creative.

  • Automated AI-driven scaffold hop identification
  • Synthesis accessibility-guided scaffold selection
  • Multi-property optimized scaffold generation
  • Integration with retrosynthesis planning tools
